# Linda's Pond

## Author
TouchReality

## Domain
Forensics

## Difficulty
Easy

## Description
Here's what's given to the players:
- Linda loves writing her poems sitting right next to her fish pond. But is it only fish that swims in her pond? Enclose your findings in kernel{}.

Two pictures. One with half of the flag hidded in a base64 encrypted format (if you look closely at the pond, maybe you'll find it). Another picture with a hidden poem. Just use the zsteg command to reveal it as so:

someone left beside the shore,
one small bowl, and nothing more.
moonlight caught a flash of gold,
even water can grow cold.
only ripples knew the name,
no one answered when I came.
every scale concealed a flame.
here a little creature stayed,
among the reeds, beneath the shade,
dreaming while the colours fade.
a keeper’s debt is never paid.

Basically just take the first alphabet of every line of the poem to form the first half of the flag. Put it all together to finally get the flag!!
